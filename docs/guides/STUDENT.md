# Studying at Globify Tech — student guide

Everything you need is at `/student` once you sign in.

---

## Getting in

You get an account when your application is approved, or when the admissions team enrols you. Check your email for the sign-in link, or use **Forgot password** at `/sign-in` if you were enrolled at the campus.

Your dashboard opens on what to do next: the lesson you stopped at, anything due soon, your next live class and anything unpaid.

---

## Learning

**Your courses** are at `/student/courses`. Open one to enter the player.

The player shows the lesson on the left and the course on the right. Video position is saved as you watch, so closing the tab does not lose your place. A lesson is marked complete when you finish it, or you can mark it yourself.

While you are in a lesson you can:

- **Take notes** that are saved against the lesson, with a timestamp if you are watching a video. They are yours; nobody else sees them.
- **Bookmark a moment** to come back to.
- **Ask the AI tutor**, which knows which lesson you are on. It is there to help you understand, so it will push you towards the answer rather than just handing it over. That is deliberate.
- **Ask the class** in the discussion thread, where your instructor and batch can answer.

**Your roadmap** at `/student/learning` shows every course you are taking, how far through you are and what is left.

---

## Assessments

**Quizzes** at `/student/quizzes`. Check the time limit and how many attempts you get before you start, because the clock does not stop once it begins. Objective questions are marked immediately; written answers go to your instructor.

**Assignments** at `/student/assignments`. Submit text, a file, a link, a GitHub repository or a live site, depending on what the instructor asked for. Late submissions may be accepted with a penalty. The due date is shown on the assignment.

**Projects** at `/student/projects` are the bigger pieces. When an instructor approves a project marked for your portfolio, it appears on your public portfolio automatically. These are what employers look at.

**Exams** at `/student/exams` are scheduled and timed. Take them somewhere with a stable connection.

---

## Classes and attendance

**Live classes** at `/student/live-classes`. The join button becomes active shortly before the class starts. Recordings appear against the class afterwards where available.

**Attendance** at `/student/attendance` shows your record per batch. Your instructor marks it, or you scan the QR code shown in class.

Attendance is usually part of finishing your course, so if you have to miss a session, tell your instructor rather than disappearing. The system flags students who go quiet, and someone will contact you.

**Your calendar** at `/student/calendar` puts classes, deadlines and exams in one place.

---

## Fees

`/student/payments` lists your invoices, what you have paid and what is outstanding.

Pay online with the methods your institute has enabled, or by bank transfer using the reference shown on the invoice. If you pay at the campus or by transfer, the finance team records it and you get a receipt in the same place.

Fees usually have to be clear before a certificate is issued. Check your balance before you finish, not after.

---

## Certificates

When you meet everything in your course's completion rule (lessons, attendance, quiz average, projects, fees) your certificate is issued. Find it at `/student/certificates`.

Every certificate has a verification code and a public page. An employer can check it at `/verify` without needing an account and without contacting us. Put that link on your CV.

---

## Career

`/student/career` shows jobs and internships from our hiring partners, ranked by how well your skills match. The match is calculated from the skills on your profile, weighted by whether they are verified, and certificates count as verification.

Apply directly. The employer moves your application through their pipeline and you are notified at each step.

`/student/portfolio` is your public page. Choose a username, write a headline, and pick which projects, skills and certificates to show. Turn it public when you are ready. This is the single most useful thing you can do for your job search here, and most students leave it until too late.

---

## Community and messages

`/student/community` is discussion across your courses and batch. Ask questions, answer other people's, mark an answer as the one that helped.

`/student/messages` is direct conversation with instructors and staff.

---

## Getting help

- Something about the lesson: the AI tutor, then the discussion thread, then your instructor.
- Something about fees: message the finance team from `/student/messages`.
- Something about your enrolment or batch: message admissions.
- Something broken: tell your instructor and say what you clicked.

`/student/settings` holds your profile, notification preferences, language and theme. Notification preferences are per channel, so you can keep the important things on WhatsApp and the rest in the app.
